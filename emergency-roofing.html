FAQ

Emergency Roofing Questions

How fast can you get here?

For emergencies we respond as fast as possible and can tarp most roofs the same day to stop further damage. Call 412-983-4397 right away.

What should I do while I wait?

Stay safe, keep off the roof, and move valuables away from any leak. Place a bucket under drips and we'll handle the rest when we arrive.

Do you work with insurance?

Yes. Storm and sudden damage is often covered — we document everything with photos to support your claim.

Do you offer permanent repairs too?

Absolutely. After we secure your roof, we schedule the full repair or replacement to fix it for good.
