FAQ

Roof Inspection Questions

Is the roof inspection really free?

Yes. Our roof inspections are completely free and come with no obligation — you get an honest report and options, and the decision is yours.

How often should I have my roof inspected?

We recommend at least once a year, and after any major storm. Catching small issues early saves you from costly repairs later.

Can you inspect a roof for a home purchase?

Absolutely. We provide a clear, documented report so you know the roof's condition before you buy.

Do you help with insurance claims?

Yes. If we find storm or sudden damage, we document it with photos to support your insurance claim.
