FAQ

Roof Repair Questions

How much does roof repair cost in Delaware?

It depends on the damage and roof type. Most minor repairs are affordable, and we give you a clear, upfront quote after a free inspection — no surprises.

How fast can you repair my roof?

For urgent leaks we respond quickly and can tarp the same day. Most repairs are completed in a single visit once we've assessed the damage.

Do you offer emergency roof repair?

Yes. If you have an active leak or storm damage, call us at 412-983-4397 and we'll protect your home right away.

Will my insurance cover the repair?

Storm and sudden damage is often covered. We inspect the roof and help you document everything for your insurance claim.
